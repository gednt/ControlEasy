<objective>
Align the ledger status-filter vocabulary and search placeholder with what the unified ledger
backend actually serves (LEDGER-02 and LEDGER-03).

Purpose: The reports page currently offers two inert status options — `Recorded` and `Refused` —
that the backend `NativeState` vocabulary never returns (the UNION emits VisitStatus
`Pending`/`CheckedIn`/`CheckedOut`/`Cancelled`/`Unknown`, PolicyOutcome `permit`/`requires_action`/
`refused`/`unknown`, refusal failure codes, and legacy `EntryState` values; there is no `Recorded`/
`Refused` string). It also shows a search placeholder promising "subject, document, or destination"
when the backend `q` filter only matches `PackageDescription`, `SubjectName`, and `Purpose`. Both
discrepancies make the surface over-promise and under-deliver.

Output: `reports.page.ts` with the inert options removed and the placeholder aligned to the
backend `q` scope, plus `reports.page.spec.ts` assertions locking the vocabulary.
</objective>
